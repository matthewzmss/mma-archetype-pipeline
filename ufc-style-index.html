UFC Style Index

Every active UFC fighter scored on 11 fighting archetypes from round-by-round UFCStats data, ranked against their own division.

Rates are recency-weighted (four-year half-life) and pulled toward the division average when a fighter has little UFC cage time, so a two-fight sample can't top a chart. Bars show where a fighter ranks in their division (50 = division median); for stats where less is better, the bar is flipped so longer always means better. Archetype fit is a 0–100 rank against all active fighters. Wrestler archetypes only score high with real takedown and control numbers behind them. All-in-One is awarded only when striking, wrestling, takedown defense and grappling are all above the division's 55th percentile.
